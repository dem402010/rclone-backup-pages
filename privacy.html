Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

About

Rclone Backup is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to back up files to a personal Google Drive account.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Rclone to access Google Drive with the owner's authorization. It may create, read, modify and delete files as needed for backup operations.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is used solely for personal backup and restore operations. When configured, Kopia encrypts backup data locally before uploading it.

Data Sharing

The owner does not sell or share Google Drive user data with advertisers or other third parties. Backup data is transferred to Google Drive for storage.

Security and Retention

Rclone stores authorization credentials locally. Backup files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the owner. The owner can revoke access through Google Account settings.
